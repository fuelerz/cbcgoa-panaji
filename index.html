Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about placing print jobs, file preparation, and branch collections.

How do I send my files for printing?

You can use our interactive WhatsApp print request form on this website. Once submitted, it opens a direct chat with your selected CBC branch where you can securely attach your PDF, CorelDraw, Illustrator, or image files.

What file formats do you accept?

We prefer high-resolution PDF files with fonts outlined or embedded. We also accept CDR, AI, PSD, JPEG, and PNG files for direct printing or prepress review.

Can I pick up my order from any branch?

Yes! We have 4 convenient branches across Goa (two in Panaji, one in Porvorim, and one in Mapusa). You can choose your preferred pickup branch directly in the print request form.

How long do standard print jobs take?

Most everyday digital prints, xerox sets, and spiral binding jobs are completed same-day. Large format flex, bulk offset orders, or specialized textured business cards may require confirmation on timeline from the branch counter.

Do you offer graphic design services?

Yes, our professional design desk assists with layout creation, visiting cards, restaurant menus, brochures, and wedding invitation prepress formatting.
